Pilates by Izzy is Izzy Samuel’s YouTube channel, and her 25-day challenge is one workout a day for 25 days. Here’s a free tracker to print and cross off as you go, and a few things worth sorting out before day 1.

V-click isn’t connected with Pilates by Izzy or Izzy Samuel. The workouts are free on her YouTube channel.

Printable 25-day tracker

Five rows of five squares, each big enough to write the day’s workout or how it went. The numbers are days of the challenge, not dates, so the sheet works whenever you start and for any season of the challenge.

Before day 1

  • Equipment. Reviews of Season 2 list a mat, light dumbbells, a small Pilates ball and a yoga block, with ankle weights optional. One day used a single heavier dumbbell.
  • A time slot. In Season 2 the workouts run from about 15 to 40 minutes. Look at the next video the evening before, so you know how much time to keep free.
  • Rest days. There’s a video for every day and no set rest day. Decide now what you’ll do when your body needs a break: a shorter video and a walk, or a day off and the next video tomorrow.
  • Level. Many of the workouts are labelled intermediate. If you’re new to Pilates, pause when you need to, change a move or use lighter weights.

If you miss a day

Do the next video tomorrow and carry on from there. The squares count challenge days, so a missed day only means you finish a day later. Write the date under Notes if you want to remember it.

After day 25

You can start the challenge again, switch to the other season or keep a lighter routine, like three workouts a week. If you want to keep a daily habit going, try a 30-day or 75-day challenge with your own rules.
